# Icons

The line icons in `res/drawable` named `t_*`, `a_*`, `ic_play`, `ic_pause`, `ic_prev` and `ic_next`
are converted from the GNOME Adwaita icon theme (symbolic set,
https://gitlab.gnome.org/GNOME/adwaita-icon-theme), used under CC-BY-SA 3.0 (or LGPL v3).
Converted to Android vector drawables with `tools/svg2vd.py`; shapes unchanged, recoloured.

The full-colour icons `res/drawable-xxhdpi/c_*.png` are rendered from the app icons of GNOME
projects (Settings, Files, Calculator, Text Editor, Music, Showtime, Papers, Software, Loupe,
Epiphany, Vocalis, Shortwave) and Easy Effects, each under its own project's licence
(CC-BY-SA 3.0 / CC0 / GPL as stated by the project). `c_more.png` is our own.
